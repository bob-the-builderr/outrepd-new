# Moments, 2026-11-10

The app celebrates a new personal best, a milestone in total push-ups
(100, 1K, 5K, 10K, 25K) and each month.

- `moments_a.html`: A. Each moment gets a screen of its own before the
  result, like LEVEL UP: confetti, the number, and a share card.
- `moments_b.html`: B. A gold card on the result screen itself.
- `moments_recap.html`: the month, the same for A and B.
  - On the first of the month, a gold card on home: "Your November is in".
  - It opens the recap: push-ups, best minute, wins and losses, longest
    streak, place among friends, and a bar for each day.

`moments.js` draws the pages with `../../2026-11-09`'s helpers. The pictures
are the `result_level_up`, `result_win`, `tab_match` and `tab_ranks_top`
screens of `ci-results`.
