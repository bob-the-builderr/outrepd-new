Privacy Policy

outrepd · Effective 27 September 2026

outrepd is a push-up app: your phone's camera counts your reps, and your set races other people's. This page says what the app keeps about you, where, and what you can do about it.

The camera stays on your phone

Pose detection runs on your device. No video and no body-position data is ever uploaded. The app may keep a recording of body positions (not video) on your phone to check the counter's accuracy; it never leaves the phone and is removed when you delete the app.

Pose detection uses Google's ML Kit library, which runs on the device. Google's ML Kit terms describe what the library itself may report to Google: developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms.

What other people can see

Your username, photo, rank, record, best set, streak, title and leaderboard places, unless you hide yourself from the boards in Settings. Anyone with your share link can see your share card without signing in. Your email address is never shown.

What we do not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of your data, no location, no contacts, no device identifiers.

Who holds the data

The app's server, database and photos are run on Supabase. When you ask for a copy of your data, it is emailed through Resend. Both process the data only to run the service.

Your data is yours

  • Get a copy: Settings → Data → Export my data.
  • Change what is shown: Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete everything: see below.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Data → Delete account. This deletes your account, profile, photo, sets, matches, friends, blocks, reports and notifications. It cannot be undone. Opponents keep the rank points they won against you, but the match disappears from their history.

If you no longer have the app, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address on your account, or with your username, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Age

outrepd is for people aged 16 and over. The app turns away anyone who says they are younger.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
